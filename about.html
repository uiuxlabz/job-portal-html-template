Our story

A job board built differently.

JobOrbit started in a co-working space with a simple observation: job boards were filled with stale listings, opaque salaries and recruiter spam. We wanted something better.

Today we serve 180,000 candidates a month and 4,200 companies. Every listing is verified before it goes live. Salaries are published upfront. Companies that receive too many complaints lose their posting privileges.

We are a small, independent team of 24 people — and we still read every support ticket ourselves.

Our values

What we stand for, every day.

Transparency first

Every listing must include a salary range. No "competitive" — we require real numbers before a post goes live.

Verified listings

Every company is vetted. Every job is checked for accuracy. Listings that misrepresent roles are removed.

Candidate-first matching

Our search ranks by relevance, not by ad spend. The best match rises to the top, not the biggest budget.
