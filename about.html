Our Story

Started With a Simple Belief

Founded in 2019, JOBENTRY began with a simple but powerful idea: the job search process should be transparent, efficient, and human-centered. Too many talented professionals were struggling to find roles that matched their skills, while companies spent months searching for the right candidates.

We built a platform that puts people first — a place where job seekers can showcase their true potential and employers can discover exceptional talent without the noise.

Today, JOBENTRY connects over 12,000 active job listings with millions of professionals across the globe, and we’re just getting started.

🎯

Our Mission

To democratize the job search experience by creating a platform where every professional — regardless of background, network, or location — can find meaningful work that aligns with their skills and aspirations.

🔭

Our Vision

To become the world’s most trusted job platform — where careers are built, companies thrive, and the future of work is shaped by opportunity, not circumstance. We envision a world where talent meets purpose.
